Open this file in a web browser, then click a link. Your browser hands the obsidian:// URI to macOS, which routes it to Obsidian. The first time, you may get a "Do you want to open Obsidian?" prompt — accept it. Clicking inside Obsidian itself or a plain terminal's open command works too.
