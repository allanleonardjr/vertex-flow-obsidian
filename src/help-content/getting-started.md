Vertex Flow brings a Linear-style task manager into your vault. Projects and
Tasks are plain Markdown notes — you can read and edit them like any other
note, and Vertex Flow keeps its own views in sync automatically.

## Install

1. Install **Vertex Flow** from the Community Plugins browser.
2. (Optional) In **Settings → Vertex Flow**, pick your preferred interface text
   size — Compact, Cozy, or Comfortable.

## Create a workspace

Workspaces are the top of the hierarchy and the only mandatory container. You
create one from the **template gallery**, which also copies in example statuses,
priorities, and types you can shape to your own workflow:

1. From the sidebar's **Workspaces** section, create a new workspace.
2. Pick a template (appropriate for a solo shopping list all the way up to a
   full team), give the workspace a name and folder, and choose an ID prefix.
3. Optionally check **populate with example content** to explore before you
   build.

## Add your first tasks

1. Add a **Project**, then a **Task** inside it.
2. Press **Enter** (or open) a task to see its editor — status, priority, type,
   labels, dates, a description, sub-tasks, and more.
3. Switch between **List** and **Board** from the view bar above the task list.

## Set up your team and vocabulary

Every workspace is self-contained. Before you go far, visit **Workspace
Settings** (`g` `s`) to shape your statuses, priorities, task types, labels,
and the People register (marking one person as **you** so `assignee:me`
filters work).

## Next steps

- Press `?` for the keyboard reference, or open **Help** in the sidebar for the
  full built-in documentation.
- Browse the **Concepts** section to understand the model.
- Build a **Saved View** to pin down the exact view you work from every day.
- Add a **Dashboard** to watch your progress at a glance.
