The List view is the dense, Linear-style heart of Vertex Flow: every task as a
row, grouped and sorted however you like. It's the most information-dense way
to see your work.

## Grouping and sorting

Rows group by **Status, Priority, Type, Assignee, Project, or Label** using the
view bar controls (or the Query bar). Groups can be collapsed and expanded.
Dragging a task from one group to another updates that field live.

## What you see

- Sub-tasks render nested under their parent, with disclosure toggles, or flat,
  or hidden — your choice per view.
- Each row shows the fields you care about, and you can **hide or show fields**
  (Type, Project, Priority, Assignee, Labels, Estimate, Start, Due, Progress,
  Relations) per view.
- Sorting is manual by default via drag-and-drop (the global `rank` order); or
  sort by any field from the view bar.

## Selection and bulk actions

List view supports multi-select — `x` to add to a selection, `⌘`/`Ctrl` + `a`
to select all, or extended arrow selection — then bulk **set status** or
**archive/unarchive** from the view bar.

## Keyboard-first

Move focus with the arrow keys or `j` / `k`, press **Enter** to open a task,
`s`/`p`/`l`/`t` to set a field on the focused task, and `?` for the full
shortcut reference. A List view like any other can be saved as part of a
**Saved View**.

## Projects

Projects live in their own tabbed editor and are managed from the **Projects**
hub — the List view concentrates on tasks. Open a project to see its tasks
filtered in place.
