title: Timeline view
order: 35
---

The Timeline (Gantt) view draws each task as a horizontal bar spanning from its
**Start** date to its **Due** date. With neither field set, the task stays in
the collapsible **Unscheduled** pane on the right until you give it a start or
a due date.

Drag a bar's body to move the whole task in time, or grab its edges to change
just the start or the due date. Tasks with both dates keep the gap between them
when you move the bar, exactly like rescheduling a chip on the Calendar.

The **zoom** control in the toolbar steps between Day, Week, Month, Quarter,
Year, and All views. Scroll or pan to move around the timeline, and the sticky
label column on the left stays put while the bars scroll.

Like every view, a Timeline can be saved as part of a Saved View — including
its zoom level — and it shares the same filters as List and Board underneath.

> Note: a dashboard widget named Timeline is a different thing — a cumulative
> area chart. This page is about the Timeline *view*.
