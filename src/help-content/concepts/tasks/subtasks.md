---
title: Sub-tasks
order: 10
---

A sub-task is a Task like any other, with a parent Task set. The parent shows a
progress bar rolled up from its sub-tasks, but nothing auto-completes or
cascades — parent and child status stay manual unless you change them yourself.

A sub-task still has its own Project field, independent of its parent. When you
create a sub-task it starts out in the same Project as its parent, but from
then on it's an ordinary field: you can point it somewhere else, and moving the
parent to a different Project won't drag its existing sub-tasks along.
