## Quick capture

**Quick capture** lets you capture a new task without leaving whatever you're
doing: run the **Quick capture: new task** command from anywhere in Obsidian
(command palette or a hotkey), and Vertex Flow creates a task in your most
recent workspace and opens it for editing. It's the fastest way to get a
thought out of your head and into the system.

## Task templates

**Task templates** let you reuse common setups instead of typing them out each
time. Templates are defined globally for the workspace, so a "Bug report" or
"Meeting follow-up" template can pre-fill a task's fields in one step.

## Bulk actions

Working with many tasks at once is supported across every list-style view:

- **Select** tasks with `x` (add to selection), `⌘`/`Ctrl` + `a` (select all),
  or extended arrow selection.
- **Set status** across the selection from the view bar.
- **Archive / unarchive** the whole selection with `e`.
- **Clear filters** to start a selection fresh.

Bulk operations act on exactly what you've selected, and archiving — like
deleting — never cascades or destroys.
